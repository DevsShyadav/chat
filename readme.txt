Plug & Play AI-powered customer support chatbot. BYOK (OpenAI/Gemini/Groq) — trains on your site content, answers customer questions 24/7.

== Description ==

**WP AI Chatbot** transforms your WordPress site into a 24/7 customer support powerhouse. Simply add your AI API key, let it train on your existing content, and watch it answer customer questions automatically.

= Why WP AI Chatbot? =

* **$29 one-time** vs $29-74/month for alternatives (Tidio, Intercom)
* **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)** — use OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Groq
* **Zero recurring costs** from the plugin itself
* **Auto-trains** on your existing WordPress content
* **Premium UI** — not your typical WordPress widget

= Features =

* Support for OpenAI (GPT-3.5/4/4o), Google Gemini, and Groq (LLaMA 3)
* Automatic content indexing from posts, pages, and custom post types
* Custom Q&A pairs for frequently asked questions
* Email fallback when AI can't answer
* Beautiful, responsive chat widget with dark mode
* Conversation history and analytics dashboard
* Rate limiting and security protections
* Customizable colors, messages, and behavior

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `wp-ai-chatbot` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu
3. Go to AI Chatbot > Settings
4. Enter your AI provider API key (OpenAI, Gemini, or Groq)
5. Go to AI Chatbot > Training and click "Re-index All Content"
6. Your chatbot is now live!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Which AI provider should I choose? =

* **OpenAI** — Best quality responses (GPT-4o recommended)
* **Groq** — Fastest responses, free tier available
* **Gemini** — Good balance of quality and cost

= How much does it cost to run? =

The plugin is a one-time purchase. API costs depend on your usage:
* OpenAI GPT-3.5: ~$0.001 per conversation
* Groq: Free tier available (limited requests)
* Gemini: Free tier for low volume

= Does it work with WooCommerce? =

Yes! Select "Products" as a content type in Training settings.

= Can I customize the chatbot responses? =

Yes. You can edit the system prompt, add custom Q&A pairs, and adjust the AI temperature for more precise or creative responses.
